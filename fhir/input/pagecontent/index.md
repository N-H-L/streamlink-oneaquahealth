### StreamLink

Citizen stream checks (Questionnaire + QuestionnaireResponse) become `preliminary` Observations
(profile `sl-citizen-observation`) with a Provenance carrying a trust assessment. After expert
verification the same Observations become `final` and also conform to the OneAquaHealth profile
`observation-indicators-oah`. Map-based baselines, lab-sampling referrals (ServiceRequest),
lab results (basedOn the referral) and feedback to the citizen (Communication) close the loop.

All example data is demo data; lab results tagged `simulated` are synthetic.
