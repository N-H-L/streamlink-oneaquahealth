# Doing one real stream check in Singapore (about 20 minutes)

Why bother: it turns "it would work anywhere" into "we did it here", and it gives the video ten honest seconds that no other entry will have. Open this page on your phone while you're there.

## 1. Pick a site (whichever is nearest)

All ten are in the app. These three are the easiest and safest, because you can see the water from a public path:

| Site | Where | Map |
|---|---|---|
| **SG2 · Kallang River, Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park** (best looking) | The naturalised river inside the park | `1.36266, 103.84353` |
| **SG1 · Sungei Ulu Pandan** | Ulu Pandan park connector, Clementi | `1.31824, 103.77189` |
| **SG8 · Bukit Timah Canal** | Alongside Dunearn Road, viewable from bridges | `1.33322, 103.79145` |

Others: SG3 Pang Sua, SG4 Api Api (Pasir Ris Park), SG5 Tampines, SG6 Punggol, SG7 Whampoa, SG9 Bedok, SG10 Geylang.
Paste the coordinates into Google Maps to get there.

## 2. Take four photos (landscape, no filters)

Stand on the path or a bridge, face the water, and take:

1. **Upstream** — looking the way the water comes from.
2. **Downstream** — looking the way it goes.
3. **The surroundings** — turn around: the path, buildings, road, whatever is beside the stream.
4. **One close-up** — anything interesting: plants on the bank, a bird or fish, litter, an outflow pipe, foam.

If you see anything unusual (foam, odd colour, a pipe discharging, works in the channel), photograph it too. That is the whole point of the app.

**Please don't:** climb down into the channel, touch the water, enter fenced or private areas, or photograph people's faces or car number plates.

## 3. Do the check in the app, at the stream

1. On your phone, open **https://n-h-l.github.io/streamlink-oneaquahealth/**
2. Tap **Check a stream** → **Use my location** (allow it) → pick the site you're standing at.
3. Answer the questions. It takes about three minutes.
   - **Answer only what you can actually see.** "Not sure" is a valid, useful answer — don't guess.
   - If the channel is concrete, say concrete. If the water is murky, say murky. Unflattering answers are the real ones.
4. On the "How it feels" step, tap **Add photo** and attach the photos. They stay on your phone; nothing is uploaded.
5. Tap **Review**. If a flag appears, either fix it or tap "It's correct".
6. Tap **Send check**, then screenshot the confirmation screen.

## 4. Afterwards

- Wash your hands.
- Copy the photos to the laptop (email or cable) and drop them in a folder; tell me and I'll add one or two to the repo with a credit line, and note the date and site.
- Tell me roughly how long the check took you and anything that was confusing or slow — that is genuine usability evidence, and it goes in the write-up.

## 5. If the site turns out to be inaccessible

Don't force it. Either move to another site from the list, or do the check from wherever you can see water and pick the nearest listed site. If the stream is dry or fenced off, answer honestly ("Dry", "Not sure") and send it anyway: an honest partial check is exactly what the trust rules are built for.
